Description

Turnkey 2-bedroom apartment of 138 m² inside Cocotal Golf & Country Club, the gated golf community in the middle of Bavaro. It is the largest 2-bedroom in our portfolio, and the only one furnished to order rather than off a catalogue.

The living and dining area is one open space, anchored by a custom wood slat TV wall with integrated lighting and open shelving. The dining table is a solid round travertine top on a carved wood base, with bouclé and walnut chairs made for the room. Wide-plank floors run throughout, and a ceiling fan keeps the double-height living area moving.

The master bedroom takes a king bed under a second ceiling fan, opens to the terrace, and connects to a walk-in closet lined floor to ceiling in cedar — bench, hanging rail and open shelving, with the aroma that comes with it. The apartment includes 2 parking spots.

It carries CONFOTUR, which means 15 years with no property tax, no transfer tax on the purchase and no capital gains tax when you sell, plus Meliá hotel benefits: beach, restaurants and spa.

Why Cocotal?

Cocotal is the gated golf community in the middle of Bavaro: tree-lined streets, the course running through it, and 24/7 security. It is not a pass-through tourist strip — people who buy here stay for long seasons or live here year-round. And with CONFOTUR on this unit, the tax saving over 15 years is a real part of the price.
