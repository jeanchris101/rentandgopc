Description

Fully-furnished 2-bedroom apartment in Costa Bavaro Garden, a modern residential community in Bavaro built around a large pool, wide lawns and internal walkways. 86 m², move-in ready, with the balcony looking straight out over the pool and gardens.

The layout is open-concept: living room, dining table and kitchen in one bright space, finished with a wood slat accent wall and tile floors throughout. The kitchen has a gas range with hood, microwave, coffee maker, a full-size refrigerator and a double sink. The master bedroom takes a queen bed; the second bedroom has two twin beds with a built-in closet and its own bathroom, plus a half bath for guests. Split A/C in the living room and in both bedrooms.

Inside the community: a resort-style pool with a separate kids pool, a fully equipped gym with cardio machines and free weights, a coworking room, a children's playground, shaded lounge and dining areas, and large green areas between the buildings. The beach is 7 minutes away and Punta Cana International Airport (PUJ) is 20 minutes.

Why Costa Bavaro Garden?

Most of what sells in Bavaro is built for the weekend tourist. This one is built for people who stay: a kids pool next to the main one, a playground, a coworking room, and enough lawn between the buildings that it stays quiet. At US$199,000 it is the lowest-priced 2-bedroom in our portfolio.
